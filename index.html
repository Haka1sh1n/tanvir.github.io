03 / Current research

Current research

Ongoing work in compute-in-memory, associative search, and nonvolatile neuromorphic circuits.

01 / Neuromorphic CIMJul 2026 – Present

TANDEM

Background Calibration and Fast Arbitration for PVT-Robust Time-to-First-Spike SRAM Compute-in-Memory. A 7 nm-class 8T SRAM-CIM architecture exploring how slow, in-situ reference calibration and fast local spike arbitration can preserve relative spike timing across PVT variation.

8T SRAM-CIMTTFS SNNTwo-pole LIFPVT resilience
Explore the architecture

Time-coded inputs feed SRAM-CIM and a tunable two-pole soma. A separate reference path provides background synapse–neuron operating-point adjustment; a pre-threshold risk monitor and provisional winner arbiter identify ambiguous spike races for selective refinement before the winner is committed. The current manuscript is being developed as TANDEM.

02 / Associative memorySep 2026 – Present

PRECIS-CAM

Energy-efficient SRAM-CAM similarity search through parity-assisted conditional sensing. Investigating a 64-bit search bank using 4-bit Hamming-distance slices, early matchline observation, and coarse-to-fine candidate evaluation.

ASAP7 7 nmSRAM-CAMHamming distancengspice
Methods & initial findings

Selective fine-stage access can avoid unnecessary detailed evaluation when coarse mismatch information is sufficient. In an initial FF-corner circuit comparison, conditional sensing reduced evaluated sensing energy by approximately 38.6% relative to forced fine sensing. This is a schematic-level result with controller energy excluded; it is not a post-layout or full-system energy claim.

03 / Emerging memoryOct 2026 – Present

HfO₂ RRAM LIF Checkpointing

A normally-off CMOS leaky integrate-and-fire neuron that checkpoints membrane voltage into a multi-level HfO₂ 1T1R RRAM cell before power gating and restores it on wake-up.

1T1R RRAMNonvolatile LIFPower gatingDevice modeling
Research questions
  • How do 2-, 4-, 8-, and 16-level RRAM states affect restoration error and SNN inference?
  • When does energy saved during idle time exceed checkpoint and restoration overhead?
  • How can selective checkpointing reduce unnecessary RRAM writes?
